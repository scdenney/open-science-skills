# Research Wayfinder

Plan the research project below as a decision map that outlives any single session — adapted for research from Matt Pocock's wayfinder. If no map exists, chart one: interview the researcher until the destination (a defensible, pre-registerable design) is concrete, write `planning/map.md` plus typed decision tickets (research / grilling / prototype / task) with blocking wired between them, fire the literature-sweep research tickets in parallel, and stop — charting is one session's work.

If a map exists, work it. Claim one open, unblocked frontier ticket and resolve it — a frontier-question interview for decisions, lookups for facts, or the matching `/oss:` skill for design work. Write the decision, the rationale, and what would change it into the ticket, then update the map's gists and frontier. Stop after one non-research ticket. When the frontier empties, hand the resolved decisions to `pre-registration-writing` to compile the pre-analysis plan.
