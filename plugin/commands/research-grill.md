# Research Grill

Interview the researcher in rounds about the idea, design, or draft below until no decision is left silently assumed. Infer the stage (`idea`, `design`, `defend`) from what they bring, or take it from the arguments; `--plan` runs the generic plan interview. Ask the whole frontier each round, numbered, each question in plain language with a one-line "why this matters" and a recommended answer that names the backing method skill. Fetch facts yourself; put every decision to the researcher. Write settled decisions to the wayfinder ticket or `decisions.md` after every round. Stop when the frontier is empty and name the next skill.
